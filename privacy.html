Chess Review – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Chess Review ("the app") is a free chess-learning app by Prishen Naidoo. This policy explains what happens with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not run a server that receives your data.

What stays on your device

Your games, analysis, puzzle progress, streaks and settings are saved only in your phone's browser storage (local storage). You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Services the app talks to

  • Chess.com public API (api.chess.com): if you type a Chess.com username, the app downloads that player's public games. Chess.com's own privacy policy applies to that request.
  • Link games with a friend (optional): uses the free PeerJS service (0.peerjs.com) only to connect two phones; the moves then go directly between the phones. Nothing is stored.
  • unpkg.com: the link-game and QR-code code libraries are downloaded from this public CDN when you first use link play.
  • YouTube links open YouTube; its privacy policy applies.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
